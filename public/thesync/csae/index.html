Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

The Sync has zero tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, distributing, or promoting any content that exploits or harms children.

  • Any violating content will be removed immediately.
  • Accounts involved in CSAE activities will be permanently banned.
  • Users can report harmful or abusive content through the app.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement authorities when legally required.
  • We actively work to maintain a safe and respectful platform.

Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates this policy, please contact us immediately.
